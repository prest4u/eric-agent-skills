# Failure Taxonomy

Use these labels when a build passes machine QA but still does not feel like a premium student book.

## Hard / Review Reject Labels

Any hard reject label is at least P1 until repaired and rerendered, unless the row explicitly names a P2 review issue.

| Label | Symptom | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `thin-cover-type` | Cover title looks light, frail, or smaller than the book identity demands. | Use a heavier title lockup; prefer B/C Chinese sans route; test at contact-sheet size. |
| `title-wrap-break` | Cover or title-page name breaks a Chinese word, splits a student name, or creates an accidental orphan such as `for Sample Learner`. | Lock the title to one line or set a deliberate editorial line break; widen the text area or reduce font size before release. |
| `single-lesson-cover-title-flat` | A single-lesson proof uses the lesson concept as text, but the cover title still reads like a generic full-course label or one-line worksheet title; Chinese words are intact but the lockup lacks editorial hierarchy. | Use a content-specific lesson title as the first signal and, when the Chinese title has a natural hinge, set an explicit editorial line break such as `高考破局 / 挑战` through `cover_title_lines` or equivalent metadata. Keep course/stage metadata quiet in kicker/title-page copy. |
| `single-lesson-cover-title-weight-weak` | A single-lesson Chinese cover uses the right title and line break, but the font/weight/scale still reads like ordinary overlay text or a thin/default CJK serif instead of a premium book-cover lockup. | Use a modern heavy CJK sans route, larger optical scale, stable contrast/shadow, and quiet metadata. At contact-sheet size the title must carry the cover, not merely label the photo. |
| `single-lesson-long-title-overflow` | A long Chinese lesson concept with a colon, list, underscore, dash, or `与` remains one cover/title line or borrows a short-title scale, causing p1/p2 horizontal overflow or a squeezed document-title look. | Split titles in the generator into explicit editorial lines, normalize list tails such as `语境 · 搭配 · 结构 · 排除`, and use a long-title optical scale. Repair the generator before batching other lessons. |
| `single-lesson-title-page-wrap-drift` | The cover uses explicit title lines but the title/navigation page ignores them, so p2 wraps differently, overflows, or looks less premium than p1. | Pass the same `cover_title_lines` metadata into the title-page renderer and inspect p2 at full-page and contact-sheet size. |
| `cover-brand-missing` | Cover lacks `Eric Teaching Studio` as a restrained bottom-right brand mark. | Add `.cover-brand` in the lower-right corner and rerender p1/contact sheet. |
| `cover-brand-low-contrast` | `Eric Teaching Studio` exists but is hard to read because it sits directly on a dark jacket, forest, animal, desk, or busy photo area without a scrim, shadow, background, or equivalent contrast treatment. | Add a restrained contrast treatment to `.cover-brand`; rerender p1/contact sheet and rerun `COVER_BRAND_CONTRAST_WEAK`. |
| `cover-double-branding` | The cover top mark and bottom-right brand both spell out `Eric Teaching Studio`, making a premium lesson/book cover feel more like a corporate template. | Keep the required bottom-right `Eric Teaching Studio` brand. On the top mark, use only a small graphic mark or a non-duplicative quiet series label; for single-lesson packs, an icon-only top mark is often stronger. |
| `cover-stage-badge` | The course stage, level, or audience such as `高二升高三` appears as a top-right cover badge. | Move stage/level metadata to title/navigation or a quiet subtitle/meta line; the cover top should not carry that badge. |
| `generic-cover-art` | The cover image is attractive but generic, reused, or not visibly connected to the current lesson/book content. | Create a content-specific cover concept; in `assets/manifest.json`, record `content_brief`, `visual_direction`, and `uniqueness_note` before ImageGen/final delivery. |
| `generic-book-identity-weak` | A no-student-name edition is only a de-personalized test build: the title is a bare functional label such as `备考计划`, `学习计划`, `workbook`, or `lesson pack`, and p1-p3 do not establish a publishable book identity. | Add `identity.cover_title`, `identity.positioning`, `identity.audience`, and `identity.front_matter_role` to `book.yaml`; rebuild cover, title/navigation, and scope map so the generic version has its own editorial identity. Machine QA reports `GENERIC_BOOK_IDENTITY_WEAK`. |
| `page-role-rhythm-weak` | A long v2 book passes page-family coverage, but the contact sheet still has extended runs of planner/final-check/exam/practice forms without an opener, article/evidence page, photo passage, handbook, or answer-key anchor. | Reduce form-only stretches and insert or redesign editorial anchors. Machine QA reports `PAGE_ROLE_RHYTHM_WEAK`; visual release must either repair it or record explicit user acceptance. |
| `page-role-variant-rhythm-weak` | A long book technically has planner/final-check pages, but many unrelated teaching moments share the same generic surface: monthly plan, daily schedule, answer sheet, listening replay, and reading review all feel like one repeated form. | Add semantic `variant` values and variant-specific renderers/row language. Machine QA reports `PAGE_ROLE_VARIANT_RHYTHM_WEAK` when long v2 books omit variants or repeat the same template+variant too densely. |
| `metadata-only-variant` | Source pages declare different `variant:` names, but the rendered HTML emits the same paper surface, no `data-surface`, no `data-surface-family`, or only numbered siblings such as `practice-1/2/3`. | Add real renderer branches and paired `data-surface-family` / `data-surface` markers; QA should fail `PAGE_STRUCTURE_VARIANT_LIBRARY_WEAK` until the visible surface changes. |
| `unit-opener-variation-weak` | Multi-unit openers all use the same accent color, same bottom objectives band, and same visual rhythm, so unit starts feel cloned even when the photos differ. | Add per-unit `opener_accent`, `opener_band`, and `opener_layout`; rotate at least two structures and three accent families in a six-unit book. Machine QA reports `UNIT_OPENER_VARIATION_WEAK`. |
| `abstract-asset-drift` | A final ImageGen cover/opener/photo/context asset becomes an abstract paper sculpture, symbolic decision board, token map, floating strip composition, or concept-art contraption that Eric cannot understand as a real scene. | Regenerate as immediately understandable real-world imagery, preferring nature, landscape, wildlife, or realistic animal photographs when suitable; otherwise use campus/classroom/library/study or modern human learning/life scenes tied to the lesson; update the manifest and rerun `FINAL_ASSET_UNINTERPRETABLE_SCENE`. |
| `nature-first-rationale-missing` | A final ImageGen/licensed visual defaults to a desk, library, campus, classroom, or modern-life scene without showing that nature/landscape/wildlife/animal imagery was considered first. | Keep the second-family scene only when it fits the content better, and add `family_rationale` or `nature_first_rationale` in the manifest; otherwise regenerate from a landscape, nature, wildlife, or realistic animal direction. Machine QA reports P2 `FINAL_ASSET_NATURE_FIRST_RATIONALE_MISSING`. |
| `cover-image-reuse` | The same image or asset id appears on the cover and again on an opener/photo-passage/context page, or multiple asset ids point to the same image file. | Treat each visual as a one-use page-role asset; generate or source separate images, set `allowed_templates`, and rerun asset gates. |
| `stacked-opener` | A unit opener is assembled from separate strips/cards, or the `Before the unit begins...` prompt floats over the image and visually collides with the Objectives band. | Make the opener one controlled composition: image, title lockup, objectives band, and aligned prompt/output blocks. Put pre-unit prompts inside `.objectives-band` as `.opener-prompt`; do not use floating `.photo-caption` text. |
| `mixed-title-scale-drift` | A single title lockup is split into unequal semantic parts, such as `IELTS备考计划` plus a smaller `for Sample Learner` inside the same H1, making the book identity feel less premium. | Keep same-title suffix/name text at the same optical scale and weight (`font-size: 1em`, inherited weight), or move it to a true subtitle/meta line instead of shrinking it inside the H1. |
| `dashboard-panel` | Page uses dark panels, metric cards, priority boxes, or app-like control areas. | Rebuild as textbook rule/practice/reference blocks with teal rules and paper surfaces. |
| `ui-number-block` | Large colored numbered squares or navigation chunks read like web/product UI. | Convert to quiet folios, section labels, or textbook route tables. |
| `control-selector-leak` | A component uses a generic selector such as `.editing-checklist label span`, so content spans, blanks, captions, or title fragments inherit control styling. In Final Check this appears as long checkbox-like rectangles where short blank lines should be. | Give UI controls explicit marker classes such as `.check-mark`; never style all descendant `span` elements in text-bearing components; add component-specific blank overrides for checklist/table/sidebar cells. |
| `component-collage` | Page is a stack of unrelated cards, strips, badges, and panels. | Redesign around one page role and one grid; remove decorative components. |
| `validation-gallery-sparsity` | A page has the correct template name, but only the first layer renders: route pages stop after a few bullets, method pages stop after one card/table, evidence pages omit the learner record, or back-matter pages show one short table with large dead space. This often happens when `pages/*.md` contains second-layer fields but the renderer ignores them. | Render the source's second-layer structures into visible book grammar: scope map, method application strip, evidence/check record, denser lookup rows, or final ticket. Starter examples must exercise these optional fields so new threads do not scaffold thin proof pages. |
| `topic-template-drift` | A page uses the right template but the row language belongs to another unit or a generic starter pattern: e.g. a listening page shows writing/rewrite categories, a health/work/topic bank uses vague "weak example" copy, or a transfer page fails to name the actual source topic and target task. | Regenerate the page data from the unit/topic role before CSS polish. Categorizing charts, vocab banks, handbook pages, and final transfer records need topic-specific rows, record prompts, and labels. A rotated generic table can pass component coverage but must not pass visual release when review residue is being cleared. |
| `a4-reading-airiness` | Article/reading pages look acceptable in book trim but become bottom-heavy on `lesson-a4`, often because the passage has paragraphs and notes but no compact task surface. | Add a short evidence/check/task strip, comprehension preview, or record surface that belongs to the reading task. Do not fill space with decorative panels; the extra A4 height should become learner action. |
| `asset-license-status-mismatch` | `assets/manifest.json` says one license family in `source_note` and another in `status`, such as a CC BY-SA image marked `licensed_final_public_domain` or a public-domain image marked CC BY. | Correct `status` and `source_note` together, then rerun `qa_textbook_pdf.py`. Machine QA reports `ASSET_LICENSE_STATUS_SOURCE_MISMATCH` as P1 because the manifest can no longer be trusted. |
| `diagnostic-form-drift` | The page uses the right textbook components, but the composition reads like a diagnostic worksheet, progress form, or workflow tool instead of a printed student book page. Common signs: too many prompt strips, form-like row cards, CJK-heavy instruction blocks, and a weak editorial bridge between activity, model, and writing surface. | Rebuild the page role from the canon: make the activity or planner one cohesive book surface, reduce tool-language prompts, restore paper texture and editorial hierarchy, and compare against golden workbook/planner/back-matter pages before tuning CSS. |
| `self-study-handbook-drift` | Eric asked for a student self-study handbook/reference manual, but the result is mostly workbook, planner, log, checklist, or fill-in surfaces. It may look polished, but the student cannot learn enough knowledge from the page without teacher explanation. | Rebuild the core rhythm around reference-book pages: one clear learnable point, explanation blocks, short examples, compact rule/table, a small quick check, and a remember strip. Keep practice pages as bridges to class tasks, not as the dominant identity. |
| `reference-book-dna-missing` | Eric cited Cambridge, English Vocabulary in Use, English Grammar in Use, National Geographic, GIC, or another professional student book, but the output still reads as generic handouts, isolated worksheets, or empty workbook pages. | Read `reference-book-style-dna.md`; rebuild around reference/practice pairs, A/B/C section rhythm, compact rule/vocabulary tables, controlled-to-personal exercise progression, real context visuals, and student-facing transfer prompts. |
| `spread-pairing-missing` | A self-study/reference page gives knowledge without a matching practice page, or gives exercises without enough reference explanation. | Pair pages or page sections as Know -> See -> Try -> Use. On A4, use consecutive pages instead of copying a book-trim spread. |
| `reference-density-weak` | A page is called a reference/handbook page but lacks explanation, model examples, compact rules/tables, quick checks, or remember/watch-out notes. | Add teachable density before adding more blank lines. The student should learn from the page without teacher explanation. |
| `image-as-decoration-drift` | A large photo or illustration is attractive but does not support the reading, listening, speaking, vocabulary, or grammar task. | Use context-specific real-world visuals and tie each image to a passage, prompt, evidence task, or language-use problem. Otherwise remove or shrink the image. |
| `copycat-reference-book-risk` | The output mirrors a named reference book too closely, including brand signals, exact layout, copied passages, exercises, screenshots, artwork, or photography. | Keep only abstract page grammar. Replace content and visuals with original/licensed material, alter layout identity, and document the source boundary. |
| `scorecard-tool-drift` | Student-visible pages name the work as `得分动作`, `动作卡`, `自检卡`, `score moves`, `scorecard`, `self-check card`, or similar tool/product labels. Even if the exercise is useful, the book starts to feel like a dashboard or coaching app rather than a premium printed student book. | Rename the page role around evidence, method, practice, and review: `In This Lesson`, `Before You Begin`, `Evidence Check`, `Opening Notes`, `Evidence Notes`, `Before You Leave`. Fix the generator/title map and validator, then regenerate; do not hand-edit one generated page. |
| `patch-drift` | Repeated local fixes create a page set that no longer has one design system. | Stop patching; restart from `visual-canon.md` and rebuild a small proof set. |
| `font-fallback-weak` | Intended font is unavailable and fallback changes weight, texture, or hierarchy. | Run font proof; choose a real installed font; document `Font decision:`. |
| `fake-editorial-serif` | Chinese serif route looks default, thin, or template-like rather than premium. | Use modern Chinese sans unless a proven strong CJK serif is installed. |
| `empty-title-navigation` | p2 title page has whitespace but no useful route, chapter map, or reading order, so it feels like a blank slide. | Add quiet editorial navigation rows; keep the page restrained but functional. |
| `loose-workbook-tail` | Activity/workbook page ends with scattered questions, isolated lines, or spare worksheet space instead of a cohesive practice record. | Build a `workbook-record` surface with header, numbered row labels, concise prompts, and bounded writing lines; machine QA should report `WORKBOOK_RECORD_SURFACE_MISSING` when this surface is absent. |
| `plain-planner-table` | Planner/review page is just a plain grid with lines, or its labels read like UI/progress controls rather than a printed workbook surface. | Wrap the planner in a paper-surface block, add an understated rule strip, soften prompt text, use editorial side labels, and keep writing lines stable. |
| `plain-final-check` | A final-check page is actually a correction/rewrite page with a checklist pasted below it, or it ends as a sparse checklist with large dead space. | Give `final-check` its own template role: thinking strip, next-step record, final checklist, and exit/timed-drill ticket so the unit ending feels like a workbook close rather than a reused form shell. |
| `thin-lesson-close-surface` | A single-lesson pack technically has review/final-check pages, but p31-style close pages end after one light record box or small ledger, leaving the lower page airy and making the proof feel like a form packet. | Add a real closure activity: thinking/check strip, review record, and an understated `下一次行动` / exit-ticket surface with stable writing lines. Repeat this at the generator/template level so every lesson closes with a printed workbook surface, not only the reported page. |
| `lesson-map-table-drift` | A single-lesson map is a two-column list or table with page-index residue, so it feels like a cropped full-book contents page rather than the opening paper surface for one lesson. | Rebuild the map as a lesson route surface with numbered anchors, soft paper field, compact task/scope rows, and no dense full-book page index. The map should answer “第XX讲怎么走” at classroom scale. |
| `same-close-surface-repeat` | Adjacent or near-adjacent close/review pages claim different functions such as 匿名热区, 私人自检, course route, or final check, but they share the same grid/card structure and only swap headings/row text. | Give each close role a real printed surface: heat/error bank cards for risk selection, route/index rows for course planning, ticket/check strip for next action, and mark the rendered HTML with a distinct `data-surface` when possible. |
| `writing-run-surface-repeat` | Consecutive writing pages such as writing 1/3, 2/3, 3/3 all render as the same sparse writing sheet with different page numbers, so the contact sheet looks like repeated forms rather than a writing process. | Split the run into distinct printed surfaces: idea/task planner, draft paper, final copy or revision/check page. Use real layout changes, not only different headings. |
| `floating-blank-line` | Fill-in-the-blank underline floats near the middle of the text line, a model-note cloze blank drops into the next line gap, a workbook-record prompt blank drops like an answer line, an activity/table/planner/checklist cloze turns into a dropped answer line, punctuation after a blank orphans, or visible exercise text shows literal underscore glyphs such as `______`. The previous global `-0.64em/-0.68em` alignment still reads too centered. | Convert underscore runs to `.blank` elements across paragraphs, questions, instructions, word-box items, activity items, tables, planner prompts, and checklist lines; bind ASCII and Chinese punctuation after blanks; use a zero-height bottom-rule blank, lower exercise `.blank` to `-0.82em` and `.question-lines .blank` to `-0.88em`, add `.paragraph-practice p .blank { vertical-align: -0.22em; }` for model paragraphs, add `.record-prompt .blank { vertical-align: -0.32em; }` for workbook prompt cloze blanks, add the inline cloze group at `-0.30em` for activity instructions/items, word boxes, tables, review rules, planner prompts, checklist labels, handbook rows, and answer tables, and do not use upward transforms such as `translateY(-2pt)`. |
| `exam-stem-slot-drift` | MCQ/cloze question stems inside `exam-mini-set` / `.guided-mcq-set` reuse generic `.blank`, literal underscores, or detached punctuation, so a prompt such as `Choose ____.` shows an underline at the wrong height or a punctuation mark floating separately. | Render question-stem placeholders through `exam_stem_text()` as `.exam-stem-slot`; wrap slot plus punctuation in `.exam-stem-keep`; keep answer writing lines and normal inline cloze blanks out of guided MCQ stems. Machine QA reports `EXAM_STEM_SLOT_DRIFT`. |
| `a4-sentence-map-table-cramp` | A4 long-sentence analysis uses a wide three-column table, squeezing the sentence and analysis columns and recreating the p16-style table pressure. | Render A4 `sentence-map` content as `.sentence-map-card-stack` with `data-surface-family="sentence-map"` and a real `data-surface`; split sentence / analysis / check into stacked fields that wrap naturally. Machine QA reports `A4_SENTENCE_MAP_TABLE_CRAMP`. |
| `a4-only-profile-residue` | A lesson-pack scoped as A4-only still contains `book-trim` profiles, book-trim output filenames, or visible strings such as `Student workbook and A4 lesson pack`, `22讲`, or `天津高考英语一轮复习`. | Generate only `student-lesson-a4` and `teacher-lesson-a4`, set `qa.output_mode: a4-only`, use visible title `高考英语复习`, and keep `天津卷 / 高二升高三 / 22讲` as internal source context only. Machine QA reports `A4_ONLY_PROFILE_RESIDUE`. |
| `visible-sanitizer-scope-leak` | A residue fix uses the same text replacement layer for student-visible prose, lesson titles, and output slugs, creating broken labels such as `第本课程`, leaving `第N讲` on an independent A4 lesson cover, or producing awkward course names. | Keep `student_text()` / `teacher_visible_text()` replacements separate from `clean_title()` and slug generation. Machine QA should treat `第本课程` and `第N讲` as `A4_ONLY_PROFILE_RESIDUE` / forbidden visible text. |
| `teacher-key-cjk-latin-fusion` | Teacher answer notes mix Chinese explanations with English phrases without spacing, making compact key tables look cramped and low-grade. | Normalize CJK/Latin boundaries in teacher-visible answers, notes, and scripts before rendering. Do this in the content generation layer, not by patching one PDF page. |
| `teacher-book-appendix-only-drift` | A file called Teacher's Book is just the student book followed by a few `Teacher Guide` / `Teacher Key` backmatter tables. | `orphan-slot-line` | In a narrow component, a blank becomes a lonely horizontal rule on its own line or splits a phrase awkwardly, such as a word-box choice `quickly ____ sold`, a handbook mini-rule `after ____`, a caption/sidebar cloze, or a narrow lookup/table/checklist cell. | Treat it as a narrow phrase blank, not a writing line: bind preceding word + blank + punctuation with `.cloze-keep`, keep word-box choices as `.word-box-item { white-space: nowrap; }`, use compact slot rules (`.word-box .blank { vertical-align: -0.26em; }`, `.handbook-rules .blank { vertical-align: -0.24em; }`), reduce slot width, wrap the whole phrase to the next row, or rewrite reference-card copy to avoid open blanks. |
| `question-line-double-blank` | A `.question-lines li` item renders both an inline `.blank` from author `____` text and one or more `.write-line` answer rules below it, creating a short floating underline plus a full answer line on paragraph-practice or photo-passage pages. | Treat `____` in question-line prompts as an authoring placeholder, not a visible cloze: render the prompt through `prompt_text_before_write_lines()` before appending `.write-line`, or remove the write-line if the task truly needs inline cloze. Machine QA must fail this as `QUESTION_INLINE_BLANK_WITH_WRITE_LINE`. |
| `planner-label-blank-weak` | A planner/check row uses a short label-prefixed blank before long prose, such as `Connector: ____ Evidence: ...`; the slot looks weak, invisible, or crowded even though `.blank` exists in HTML. | Remove the inline placeholder from the prompt and use the row's dedicated writing lines for the answer. If an inline cloze is truly required, split it into a separate compact field rather than sharing a line with long explanatory text. |
| `weak-backmatter` | Handbook/reference/answer-key page is sparse, low-density, or reads like another worksheet/summary page instead of lookup back matter. | Use pale blue-gray field, a top meta band, numbered compact index rows, quiet section labels, mini rules, grouped answer-key blocks, and a denser reference table rhythm. |
| `rendered-text-glitch` | Rendered PNG text has glyph ambiguity, broken punctuation spacing, or mixed CJK/Latin/digit shaping that extracted text does not reveal. | Inspect full-page PNGs; rewrite ambiguous ranges such as `11-14` when needed, fix spacing, or choose a safer font before scoring. |
| `student-process-language` | Student-visible copy contains backstage terms such as `Route`, `主动作`, `维修`, `后台`, or `闭环`. | Rewrite with concrete learner actions and run the teaching-polish strict scan on extracted PDF text. |
| `visible-title-ai-language` | Student-book/workbook page headings expose Chinese production-role labels such as `路线`, `微技能训练`, `段落诊断`, `作业说明`, or `复盘记录`, or English-but-AI labels such as `Lesson Route`, `Lesson Rhythm`, `Practice Rhythm`, or `Micro Skills`, making the page feel like a generated teaching workflow rather than a printed workbook. | Add a generator-level visible-title map and render workbook English headings such as `Lesson Map`, `Writing Moves`, `Paragraph Diagnosis`, `Homework Practice`, and `Review Record`; rerun heading scans and visual QA. |

## Common False Positives

- A page can have boxes and still be book-like if the boxes are rule tables, word boxes, checklist rows, or writing planners.
- A page can use strong color and still be restrained if color is doing textbook navigation work.
- A cover can be quiet and still be premium, but the title lockup must remain strong enough to read as a book identity.
- A title can intentionally use two lines, but accidental browser wrapping is never an editorial lockup.
- Starter content is valid only inside the original starter/golden sample; in a Sample Learner/custom project it is source contamination, even when it is hidden in `tools/build.py`.

## Contact-Sheet Diagnosis

When reviewing a contact sheet, scan in this order:

1. Does the cover read as a book at thumbnail size?
2. Does the cover/title lockup keep Chinese words and names intact?
2a. For a single-lesson pack, does the cover title have a lesson-specific editorial lockup rather than a flat course label or one-line worksheet title?
2b. If the lesson concept is long, do p1 and p2 share explicit title lines and avoid overflow/squeezed H1 drift?
3. Does the cover place `Eric Teaching Studio` bottom-right with enough contrast to read over the photo, without a top-right level/stage badge?
3a. Does the cover avoid duplicate Studio wordmarks, especially in single-lesson packs where the top mark can be icon-only?
4. Does the cover image feel specifically designed from this lesson/book content rather than a generic reusable prompt?
4a. If this is a no-student-name edition, does it have a real publishable book identity rather than a stripped personalized title or bare plan/workbook label?
5. Are final generated visuals real-world and immediately understandable, not `abstract-asset-drift` paper/symbol/token compositions?
6. If a final visual uses the second family (school/study/modern life), does the manifest explain why it fits better than the preferred nature/landscape/wildlife/animal direction?
7. Is the cover image used only once, with opener/photo/context visuals using separate images?
8. Are at least five page roles visibly different?
8a. In a 24+ page v2 book, are there editorial anchors often enough that the contact sheet does not become a long form-only run?
8b. Do planner/final-check pages have semantic variants that match their real teaching role, rather than one generic form repeated under different titles?
8c. Do unit openers rotate accents and at least two structures, or do they look like cloned photo pages with the same color band?
9. If this is a student-book form conversion, do article/opener, skill/method, practice, diagram/review, and handbook roles all appear rather than one repeated exercise-list form?
9a. If this is a self-study learning handbook, are there real reference pages with explanations, examples, compact rule tables, quick checks, and remember strips, rather than a contact sheet dominated by workbook/log/planner/checklist surfaces?
9b. If Eric asked for Cambridge/In Use/National Geographic/GIC-style work, does the contact sheet show reference/practice pairing, A/B/C section rhythm, controlled-to-personal exercise progression, and context visuals that serve the task?
9c. Is the result safely abstracted rather than a clone of a named book's brand, passages, exercises, images, screenshots, or exact spreads?
10. Do article/concept pages have anchored visual/evidence fields and paragraph rhythm, not just a title over notes?
11. Do skill/practice pages use section ribbons and cognitive labels where the form calls for them?
12. Do the dense pages look like textbook explanation/practice, not dashboards?
13. Do workbook pages have a cohesive record/practice surface rather than loose question tails?
14. Do activity, planner, and record pages feel like printed workbook surfaces rather than diagnostic forms or production tools?
14a. Do single-lesson route and close pages have enough printed surface depth: lesson map, review record, and next-action/exit-ticket rhythm, rather than thin tables with dead space?
15. Does back matter switch into lookup/reference rhythm, including grouped answer-key hierarchy when an answer key is present?
15a. If this is a teacher book, can a teacher run the class from the page order, with teacher notes and answer references near the matching student-equivalent page, or is all teacher value appended at the back?
16. Do inline fill-in blanks sit in the correct optical class: answer-line blanks near the lower writing baseline, paragraph/model cloze blanks with paragraph text, record-prompt cloze blanks with prompt text, activity/table/planner/checklist cloze blanks as inline cloze, and word-box / mini-rule / caption / sidebar blanks as compact no-wrap phrase slots rather than orphaned horizontal rules? Are there no literal underscore glyphs, orphaned ASCII/CJK punctuation after blanks, or weak label-prefixed planner blanks?
17. Are title weights, suffix/name scale, and Chinese glyphs strong enough across pages, with no `mixed-title-scale-drift`?
18. Do checkbox markers, badges, and other UI-like primitives avoid leaking their styling onto content spans or blanks?
19. Are rendered digits, punctuation, apostrophes, and mixed Chinese/English strings visually unambiguous in the PNGs?
20. Is every visible/source identity from the requested project, not the starter sample?
21. Are text-heavy pages selectable/searchable rather than rasterized?

If the answer to any of the first three questions is no, do not write a visual PASS.
