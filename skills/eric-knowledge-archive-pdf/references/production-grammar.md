# Production grammar

## Canonical sequence

1. Cover/accession: one dominant object plate, vertical or detached title logic, catalogue code, restrained thesis.
2. Condition record: macro object detail with local annotations and a separate accession-data panel.
3. Collection context: conservation work surface linked visibly to outcomes and stewardship intent.
4. Cataloguing method: non-uniform sequence or axis; never a generic table or dashboard.
5. Deposit sequence: materially distinct specimens mapped to stages, custody, version, and responsibility.
6. Closure/boundary: dark field for engagement and governance, followed by a light deposited-assets register.

## Colour grammar

Use one dark structural primary, one low-saturation secondary, and one muted signal colour. Treat warm paper as a neutral rather than a fourth semantic colour. Repeat colour through rules, accession codes, and image tonality, but pair every signal with type, numbering, geometry, or labels. For a knowledge archive, prefer charcoal, paper beige, dark moss, and a restrained ochre signal. Images should retain plausible clay, paper, cloth, stone, or metal tonality.

## Typography and geometry

Use a display serif for accession numerals and English editorial echoes, a book serif for Chinese record text, and a neutral sans for labels and metadata. Keep body text at approximately 9–10 pt on A4, labels at approximately 7.5–8.5 pt, and hairlines no thinner than about 0.3 mm. Use asymmetric fields, fine rules, vertical accession elements, and controlled negative space. Avoid repeated card grids.

## Image and evidence contract

Give every image a unique page role. Record asset id, source or generation, prompt or deterministic processing, page role, pixel dimensions, text policy, and file hash. Images must contain no visible text, labels, logos, watermarks, answer marks, or interface elements. After the final edit, build a new PDF, render pages from that PDF, create a contact sheet, run structural and layout checks, inspect key pages at full size, and freeze exact hashes.
