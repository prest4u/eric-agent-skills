# Strategic advisory visual and production grammar

## Palette roles

- Primary: deep navy for authority fields, closing fields, and major structural anchors.
- Secondary: cobalt for transitions, ordered movement, markers, and image midtones.
- Signal: restrained blue-green for one or two consequence/boundary cues.
- Neutrals: cool white and cool grey for reading fields, captions, and inactive structure.

Do not introduce another chromatic family. Keep body text near-navy, never pale blue. Avoid large saturated fills behind small text. Check grayscale legibility so colour never carries meaning alone.

## Composition

- Use asymmetric editorial fields rather than centred pitch-deck layouts.
- Alternate low-density thesis pages with dense method and engagement pages.
- Maintain continuous rails, seams, folds, and rule systems; avoid floating cards and UI panels.
- Use architectural or abstract material images as evidence of threshold, sequence, dialogue, or responsibility.
- Crop each image for its page role and use it once only.

## Typography

- Use a firm modern sans for metadata, labels, stages, and folios.
- Use a dignified Chinese serif for proposition and closing language when available.
- Keep body text at or above 8.7 pt and metadata at or above 7.5 pt.
- Preserve generous line spacing, embedded fonts, searchable text, and explicit reading order.

## Print and PDF controls

- Fix A4 page dimensions in CSS and prevent accidental pagination.
- Export with backgrounds enabled and no external resources.
- Verify qpdf, pdfinfo, pdftotext, pdffonts, and PDF-origin renders.
- Reject JavaScript, blank pages, low-resolution images, clipped text, accidental masking, and stale renders.
- Freeze hashes only after the final source, PDF, renders, contact sheet, provenance, and Skill metadata are complete.
