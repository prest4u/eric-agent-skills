# Research Monograph Visual and Production Grammar

## Page sequence

Use this six-role sequence as a starting grammar, adapting length to the evidence:

1. State the research proposition through a dominant thesis and one structural figure.
2. Translate visible figure features into clearly labelled interpretive relations.
3. Name the research question, working thesis, authorised corpus, and exclusions.
4. Trace each claim to a source unit and label its evidence status in words.
5. Show the synthesis protocol: quote, link, bound, conclude.
6. State the conclusion, limitations, and evidence still required.

Do not turn this into a consulting journey, dashboard, card wall, policy dossier, or plain table sequence.

## Visual system

- Use A4 portrait with a 12-column editorial grid and at least 13 mm live margins.
- Keep horizontal running heads and citation feet as the persistent apparatus.
- Alternate sparse figure pages with dense corpus, trace, method, and limitation pages.
- Use Chinese-first serif display type, sans body text, and monospaced labels.
- Keep body text at 9 pt or larger and metadata above 7.6 pt.
- Use documentary object/material imagery only when it clarifies a page role. Keep each image unique to one page.
- Keep text searchable; never rasterize text-heavy pages.

## Colour system

Keep ink and paper dominant. Use one dark indigo primary, one cool-grey secondary, and one muted blue-grey signal. Apply colour to figure tonal ranges, evidence status, source keys, leaders, chapter markers, and sparse rules. Repeat every evidence distinction in text, numbering, line style, or position so colour is never the only carrier of meaning. Check contrast in colour and grayscale print previews.

## Evidence discipline

- Declare the authoritative corpus and exclusions before synthesis.
- Label claims as direct, synthesised, bounded, or another explicit status.
- Connect every image annotation to a visible feature; state when a figure is explanatory rather than evidentiary.
- Separate conclusion, limitation, and next-evidence fields.
- Record asset source/generation, prompt or transform, page role, dimensions, no-text policy, unique use, and hash.

## Production and validation

Build from deterministic HTML/CSS or another searchable-text route. Export a new derivative PDF, render every page from that PDF, and create a fresh contact sheet. Verify page count and A4 geometry, searchable text, embedded fonts, no JavaScript, no external requests, image resolution, and artifact freshness. Audit the browser layout for overlap, clipping, masking, overflow, console errors, and page errors. Inspect the cover, all image pages, the densest page, the method/data page, and final page at full size. Freeze PDF, page-render, contact-sheet, source, provenance, QA, and Skill hashes before independent review.
