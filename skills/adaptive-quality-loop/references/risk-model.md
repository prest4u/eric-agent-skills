# Impact and verification

Identify the specific acceptance claim and the failure that could invalidate it. Host and task policy decides required review and authority; this reference is not an automatic risk classifier or a document-count score.

| Situation | Useful evidence or action |
|---|---|
| Clear, reversible local change or draft | Produce the result and gather sufficient targeted evidence |
| A consequential assumption could invalidate costly work | Make a representative reversible result and resolve the material uncertainty |
| External delivery, sensitive change, migration, destructive action, deployment, critical behavior, or explicit sign-off | Preserve the host's authority, recovery, evidence, and independent review requirements |

A new project, multiple files, a rendered document, or a subjective quality adjective alone does not create a formal record or an independent review. Apply the actual project/host requirements for shared behavior and critical interactions.

Missing evidence blocks the claim or action that depends on it. Return completed local work with its actual gaps. Never describe an unreviewed release as ready, remove a required safety check, or broaden a task to satisfy a generic checklist.
