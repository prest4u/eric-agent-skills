# Video Content Review Lens

Use this for video scripts, titles, covers, hooks, storyboards, shot lists, and short-form content drafts.

## Required Checks

- Audience: target viewer, pain point, and desired action are specific.
- Promise: title/cover/opening promise is clear and deliverable by the script.
- Hook: first 5-15 seconds create a reason to keep watching; avoid generic throat-clearing.
- Retention: sequence has contrast, examples, payoff, and pattern breaks.
- Spoken rhythm: sentences are speakable, short enough, and free of stiff written transitions.
- Production feasibility: visuals, screen cuts, captions, B-roll, demos, or examples are marked where needed.
- Platform risk: exaggerated claims, absolute language, compliance-sensitive wording, or unsupported promises are flagged.

## Severity Calibration

- P0: false or risky claim, title/cover promise not supported, platform-sensitive violation likely to block publishing.
- P1: weak hook, unclear audience, no payoff, script cannot be recorded naturally.
- P2: improve pacing, examples, visual beats, caption emphasis, or closing CTA.
- P3: phrase-level polish.

## Evidence Requirements

Cite title, cover text, timestamp/section, exact script lines, or missing production notes. If judging spoken rhythm, quote the stiff phrase and offer a speakable replacement.

## Reviewer Non-Scope

Do not review code correctness or teaching source authenticity unless the video makes those claims.
