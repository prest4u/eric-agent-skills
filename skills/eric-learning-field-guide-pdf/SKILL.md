# Eric Learning Field Guide PDF

## Core outcome

Create an adult field guide that moves from observation to practice and leaves a reusable record. Keep the PDF editorial and tactile: live text, real learning materials, clear page roles, modest colour, and ample writing or reflection space.

## Workflow

1. Lock the audience, field situation, six-to-eight page sequence, output filename, and no-overwrite policy.
2. Read [references/visual-grammar.md](references/visual-grammar.md) before designing or adapting the route.
3. Preserve one observable learner move per page: orient, observe, trace evidence, decide, practise, transfer, reflect, or reuse.
4. Use HTML/CSS or another deterministic text engine. Keep headings, instructions, labels, captions, and records searchable; never rasterize a text-heavy page.
5. Use real workshop, study, material, or pathway imagery with no visible text. Give every image one unique page role and record its source or generation prompt, transform, dimensions, text policy, and unique use.
6. Apply one primary, one secondary, and one signal colour. Keep body text and structural rules professionally dark, and never make colour the only carrier of meaning.
7. Build a fresh A4 PDF, render every page from that exact PDF, make a contact sheet, and inspect the cover, all image pages, the densest method/data page, and the final page at full size.
8. Verify six or the contracted page count, A4 size, searchable text, no JavaScript, no external requests, and zero overlap, clipping, masking, overflow, console errors, or page errors.
9. Freeze the exact PDF, contact sheet, page renders, SKILL.md, and `agents/openai.yaml` hashes. Leave formal final approval to an independent reviewer.

## Content grammar

- Start with a real work or learning situation, not software features.
- Phrase tasks as observable actions and named outputs.
- Pair methods with a worked record, boundary, or decision example.
- Close with reflection plus a reusable artefact: field note, facilitation record, pathway, SOP input, or programme handoff.
- Keep governance and privacy boundaries concrete when the field guide involves organisational AI.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not use dashboard cards, app controls, decorative data panels, or colour-only categories.
- Do not reuse one image across pages or place teaching text inside generated imagery.
- Do not use paid assets, private uploads, copied reference-book expression, or unlicensed fonts.
- Do not overwrite an accepted PDF; create a named derivative.
- Do not mark the frozen candidate as independently approved in the writer pass.
