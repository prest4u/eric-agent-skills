## Project Direction

Series name:

```text
大问题里的哲学史
```

Subtitle:

```text
用哲学故事训练现代人的判断力
```

Core formula:

```text
现代困境
-> 历史场景
-> 哲学人物
-> 一个核心概念
-> 一个常见误读
-> 一个现实判断方法
```

Do not start from "今天讲康德/庄子/尼采". Start from a modern dilemma.

## Project-Specific Gates

- Keep the visual system: "思想档案馆 + 现代困境批注".
- Avoid blue/purple tech gradients, black-gold masterclass style, wellness glow, and lecture PPT.
- Mark missing historical portraits, archive images, BGM, SFX, and voiceover as placeholders or fallbacks.
- Do not treat historical portraits, archive images, BGM, SFX, generated images, or voiceover as publish-ready until rights/source/privacy status is recorded in the project.
- Keep generated/concept images distinct from historical evidence; never present AI-generated visuals as archival material.
- Every scene must carry a judgment payload, not only mood.
- After render, `video-qa` must prove visible frames; after that, `eric-review` must check philosophy boundary and non-chicken-soup tone.
