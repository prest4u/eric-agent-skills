# Visual and Production Grammar

## Six-page rhythm

1. **Paced interruption:** open with a vertical image portal, a short reading interval, and the title anchored low and right.
2. **Case question:** let an image open a large question field; use a compact case index and one belief statement.
3. **Dialogue positions:** arrange three responsibility positions around a shared question; avoid panels, cards, or a dashboard grid.
4. **Seminar interval:** place seven method steps along a single listening axis; alternate annotations rather than forming a checklist table.
5. **Case-room deliberation:** use one asymmetric exchange path for discover, diagnose, design, and pilot; keep the deliverables attached to the discussion.
6. **Organizational transfer:** stage the closing image above scope, data, judgment, and one transfer prompt.

Each turn must change the dominant title anchor, tonal field, and image–text relationship. Do not repeat one page skeleton.

## Colour system

- Primary: midnight blue `#14213D` for major headings, kickers, and image shadows.
- Secondary: dark burgundy `#5B1E2D` for interventions, labels, and selected image midtones.
- Signal: soft bronze `#B08A57` for hairlines, progress markers, and sparse image highlights.
- Paper neutrals: `#FBFAF7`, `#F1EEE8`, and `#E7E3DC`; body ink `#111319`.

Keep saturation low to medium. Use colour to support hierarchy, never as the only carrier of a distinction. Make labels readable by wording and position as well as hue.

## Imagery

Use restrained, text-free imagery that reads as an executive learning intervention rather than decoration. Suitable structures include listening rings, stone or material forms, quiet horizons, and case-opening intervals when the content calls for abstraction. Keep generated or transformed assets free of visible text, pseudo-letters, logos, watermarks, answer marks, and UI symbols.

Use each image exactly once. Assign a distinct role such as cover portal, case opening, method axis, or transfer horizon. Record source path/hash, output path/hash, prompt or deterministic tonal transform, page role, dimensions, colour mapping, and text policy.

## Typography and composition

Use an old-style serif for reflective display lines and a clean Chinese sans for body text. Keep body type at or above 8.5 pt. Use whitespace as pacing, not as empty template space. Prefer fine bronze rules and live labels to boxes, badges, or interface components.

Reject dashboard panels, UI number blocks, component collage, accidental title wrapping, body-text rasterization, repeated image frames, weak contrast, fake text in imagery, clipping, masking, overflow, or a contact sheet dominated by one repeated structure.

## Evidence

Require a six-page A4 searchable PDF with no JavaScript, six fresh PDF-origin PNGs, a fresh colour contact sheet, browser geometry and console evidence, asset provenance, full-size inspection notes, and frozen SHA-256 hashes. A writer may report candidate evidence but must leave final independent approval to another reviewer.
