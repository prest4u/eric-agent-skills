# News, Social, And Entertainment Delta

Apply this delta to news, viral posts, rumors, public-figure or entertainment claims, creator disputes, media provenance, anonymous reports, edited media, and fast-moving narratives.

- Split background facts, the disputed claim, detail claims, provenance claims, and interpretations. A verified background fact cannot certify an unverified viral detail.
- Trace the earliest reachable original post, official statement, direct recording, agency or court record, platform source, or first report.
- Record original URL, earliest reachable time, source role, edit/deletion state, translation, crop, caption, screenshot or repost chain, and independent confirmation when material.
- Do not count multiple outlets repeating one origin as independent evidence. Treat reposts, screenshots, anonymous entertainment reports, fan accounts, captions, translations, and short clips as leads until verified.
- Preserve a dated update sequence. Later evidence may change current status but must not erase the original claim or historical state.
- Do not treat lack of comment as confirmation or denial. If an original source is deleted, blocked, or provenance cannot be inspected, use `unverifiable` and name the next recovery action.
