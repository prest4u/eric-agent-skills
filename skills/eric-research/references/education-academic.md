# Education And Academic Delta

Apply this delta to learning science, academic papers, literature reviews, citation checks, education policy, curriculum, teaching methods, and learning-effect claims.

- Prefer full original papers, DOI or registry records, official datasets, government or institutional policy, curriculum standards, and official reports. Use systematic reviews within their inclusion criteria.
- Inspect the exact passage, table, figure, or policy clause. A real or topically related citation is not enough; record whether it directly, partially, or does not support the claim.
- Separate recommendation, association, causal result, implementation condition, and generalization. Never convert guidance or an average effect into a universal guarantee.
- Record learner group, age/grade, subject, setting, intervention, comparator, duration, outcome, study design, jurisdiction, and applicability boundary when material.
- Distinguish engagement, satisfaction, self-report, accuracy, test scores, transfer, and long-term outcomes.
- For current policy, use the official jurisdictional source and preserve draft, effective, replaced, and superseded states.
- Do not infer a company's demand or financial health from learning evidence; apply the marketing or finance delta to those separate claims.
