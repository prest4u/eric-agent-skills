# Marketing Delta

Apply this delta to market size, competition, positioning, customer segments, pricing, demand, advertising, growth channels, app-store or SEO evidence, and marketing strategy claims.

- Freeze category, geography, audience, buyer versus user, time window, and decision use before generalizing.
- Separate vendor self-representation, independent market evidence, customer signals, pricing, public-company evidence, and estimates.
- A vendor page may verify only the vendor's own stated positioning or price. It cannot by itself verify market demand, share, uniqueness, efficacy, independence, or category leadership.
- For size, growth, adoption, downloads, users, pricing, or demand, record method, date, geography, population, units, exclusions, and whether the figure is observed, modeled, or estimated.
- Treat app listings, reviews, forums, social posts, ad libraries, and SEO visibility as bounded qualitative signals with explicit sample limits.
- Separate observation from positioning judgment. Link recommendations to claim IDs and cap certainty at the weakest supporting evidence.
- Do not infer learning effectiveness or financial health from market evidence; apply the education or finance delta to those separate claims.
