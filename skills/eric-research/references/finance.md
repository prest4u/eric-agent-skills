# Finance Delta

Apply this delta to companies, filings, earnings, guidance, revenue, valuation research, macro data, regulators, and material financial claims.

- Prefer filings, regulator records, official investor-relations releases, central banks, treasuries, exchanges, and source statistical agencies. Trace every material current key number to S1.
- Record entity, fiscal or data period, publication/update date, currency, units, accounting or adjustment basis, and whether the number is actual, guidance, estimate, consensus, or rumor.
- For `latest` or `current`, compare both publication date and fiscal/data period; preserve revisions and amended filings.
- Never represent guidance, estimate, consensus, or rumor as actual performance. Preserve GAAP/non-GAAP, nominal/real, segment-definition, units, and currency differences.
- Use media and analyst summaries as context or cross-checks, not as the controlling source when an official source exists.
- Provide neutral evidence, assumptions, methods, and scenarios. Do not tailor buy/sell/hold advice to Eric's holdings, finances, or risk tolerance.
