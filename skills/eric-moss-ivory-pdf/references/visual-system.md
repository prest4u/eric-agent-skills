# Eric Moss Ivory PDF｜苔绿象牙

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#F6F3EA` | Warm ivory page |
| Ink | `#252824` | Titles and body |
| Moss | `#626A56` | Primary structure |
| Slate | `#5D6C75` | Secondary analytical cue |
| Muted | `#747568` | Subtitles and supporting copy |
| Micro | `#646659` | Footers and microtext |
| Hairline | `#D3D2C6` | Quiet divisions |

Target an approximate 70:30 moss-to-slate emphasis ratio. The page is warm ivory, not yellow.

## Typography

| Role | Preferred | Safe fallbacks | Typical size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover title | Songti SC Bold | STSong, Noto Serif CJK SC, Source Han Serif SC | 34–38 pt |
| Section title | Songti SC Bold | same serif fallbacks | 23–26 pt |
| Closing statement | Songti SC Regular/Medium | same serif fallbacks | 17–20 pt |
| Body | Songti SC Regular | same serif fallbacks | 10–10.5 pt |
| Lead | PingFang SC Regular | Hiragino Sans GB, Noto Sans CJK SC | 11–12 pt |
| Pull quote | STFangsong | FangSong, Songti SC | 14–17 pt |
| Labels | Avenir Next + PingFang SC | Helvetica Neue + Noto Sans CJK SC | 7.2–8 pt |
| Footer | PingFang/Avenir | sans fallbacks | 7–8 pt |

Use no more than these role families. Keep body weight regular and display weight bold rather than black.

## Accent Responsibilities

- Moss: Chinese structure, primary rails, key quotations, first-order labels.
- Slate: English eyebrows, secondary categories, inference/check labels, and the final third of split rules.
- Do not place moss and slate as equal fills inside the same small component.
- Use ink for body meaning; accents organize rather than decorate.

## Layout

- A4 portrait.
- Default margins: top 24 mm, bottom 22 mm, left/right 25 mm.
- Editorial rail: about 16 mm plus a 10 mm gutter.
- Body line leading: approximately `0.9–0.98em` in Typst.
- Prefer 0.5–0.9 pt rules.
- Keep at least one-third of low-density pages visually open.

## Character

Warm, reflective, tactile, and calm. It should feel like premium uncoated paper, not a sepia filter or beige lifestyle template.

## Reject

- Bright yellow, tan, kraft, or saturated olive.
- Moss and slate fighting for equal attention.
- Filled cards, gradients, stock-paper textures, or decorative botanical motifs.
- Accent color applied to whole paragraphs.
- Hairlines too pale to survive ordinary grayscale printing.
- A centered quotation on every page.
