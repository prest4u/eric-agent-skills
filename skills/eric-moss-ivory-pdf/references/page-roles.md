# Page Roles

Select roles from meaning, not page count. A long document should change rhythm when the reader's task changes.

| Role | Use when | Structural cue | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover | Establish identity and reading promise | Large title, editorial rail, minimal subtitle, quiet lower metadata | Contents, summaries, decorative imagery |
| Section opener | Begin a major idea or chapter | Large section numeral, short premise, 2–3 orientation terms | Full essay density |
| Continuous essay | Sustain close reading | One primary column, optional narrow side note, generous paragraph rhythm | Cards or repeated subheadings |
| Argument / contrast | Explain two plausible paths or positions | Two columns or aligned pairs, one restrained quotation | Treating every contrast as good versus bad |
| Evidence / judgement | Help the reader distinguish fact, inference, and motive claims | Evidence rows, paired criteria, explicit verification questions | Dense tables, badges, traffic-light colors |
| Numbered process | Present ordered actions, principles, or stages | Quiet side numerals, one sentence of explanation per step | UI chips, timelines with decorative nodes |
| Transition / quotation | Create a deliberate pause between dense sections | One literary quotation, large whitespace, short context note | Using this role repeatedly |
| Conclusion | Compress the document into a durable final frame | 2–3 anchors, one closing statement, low-density close | Repeating the entire document |
| Sources / notes | Preserve provenance or bounded caveats | Compact rows, hairline divisions, small but printable text | Raw URLs across the page, technical file paths |

## Rhythm Rules

- Documents of 1–3 pages may use cover plus one or two content roles.
- Documents of 4–7 pages should use at least two content roles.
- Documents of 8+ pages should use at least four roles, including a distinct conclusion.
- Do not repeat the same title/lead/quote/note stack on consecutive pages unless the content requires a deliberate series.
- Let long prose continue without artificial components. Introduce structure only when it changes the reader's action.

## Role Variants

- Continuous essay: full-width, main-column-plus-side-note, or two-column only for short paragraphs.
- Argument: mirrored columns, claim/evidence pairs, or a left-rail thesis with supporting rows.
- Evidence: fact/inference/check rows, two-sided criteria, or a verification ladder.
- Process: numbered vertical list, grouped principles, or three-stage compression.
- Conclusion: three anchors, one long closing statement, or a short letter-like close.

## Cross-Theme Test

Use identical content and identical roles in both official themes. A theme may change color tokens and restrained accent distribution; it should not require different line breaks, page counts, or component geometry.
