# Project archetype seeds

These are question sets and possible starting metaphors, not reusable skins. Change them whenever the product’s content suggests a stronger language.

## Decision and comparison tools

Ask how alternatives, constraints, uncertainty, and personal context should be understood without implying a ranking.

Possible seeds: branching horizons, parallel dossiers, converging field lines, scenario rooms. Avoid using height, brightness, or forward position as an accidental recommendation signal.

## Measurement and evidence tools

Ask what is observed, withheld, incomplete, provisional, or disabled. Make uncertainty legible without turning metadata into a score.

Possible seeds: signal constellation, calibrated instrument bench, evidence ledger, layered aperture. Incomplete or withheld states must remain first-class.

## Narrative and life-history products

Ask what should feel continuous, remembered, revisited, or unresolved. Let emotional cadence determine motion rather than applying spectacle everywhere.

Possible seeds: time corridor, memory atlas, weather layers, inhabited archive. Preserve quiet states and readable long-form content.

## Editorial and world-view products

Ask how context, evidence, geography, impact, and reader navigation relate. Avoid making serious material feel like a game or financial terminal.

Possible seeds: editorial observatory, issue field, layered world lens, evidence map. Favor hierarchy, source clarity, and calm transitions.

## Enterprise decision products

Ask what evidence supports action, hold, escalation, or rerun. Make the consequence and the safe next action visually unambiguous.

Possible seeds: decision chamber, operational gates, evidence architecture, controlled flow field. Preserve HOLD and non-send semantics.

## Spatial games and interactive worlds

Ask whether the requested improvement belongs in world rendering, character motion, camera, feedback, or interface. Do not impose landing-page scroll grammar on play.

Possible seeds: world-space lighting language, material zones, character-state animation, environmental storytelling. Runtime feel, collision, frame pacing, and input response outrank decorative UI.
