# Technical atlas visual and production grammar

## Page-role sequence

| Role | Composition | Colour role |
|---|---|---|
| Cover cutaway | Large technical object plus datum rail and restrained thesis | Material colour carries identity; title stays dark |
| Proposition aperture | Narrow full-height image with four anchored callouts | Navy field, copper codes, orange targets |
| System relationship | Central structural node connected to four named responsibilities | Navy core; labels and geometry carry meaning |
| Exploded method plate | Central exploded assembly with steps around its perimeter | Steel and copper object; copper step codes |
| Delivery evidence | Wide calibrated specimen band plus six outputs | Three labelled callouts; orange is reserved for the final signal |
| Governance decision | Process spine, operating boundaries, dark final decision band | Navy decision field; copper/orange progression markers |

## Tokens

- Primary: deep navy `#12344A`
- Secondary: copper `#A6673D`
- Signal: orange `#E66528`
- Ink: dark blue-black `#102331`
- Steel neutrals: `#465761`, `#71808A`, `#DCE2E3`
- Paper: low-ink warm white `#F8F7F3`

Use colour at low-to-medium saturation for print. Maintain adequate contrast in grayscale. Do not assign meaning through colour alone.

## Typography and geometry

- Use a heavy modern sans for Chinese display titles, a neutral sans for body text, and a mono face for codes and datum labels.
- Keep A4 at `210mm × 297mm`, zero browser margins, fixed page boxes, and explicit page breaks.
- Use datum rails, hairlines, measured apertures, leader lines, and printed folios. Avoid app chrome, floating cards, controls, and dashboard grids.
- Keep body text at or above 8.6 pt unless a specific print test proves a smaller size.

## Deterministic colour adaptation

Map luminance into deep-navy shadows, steel-grey midtones, and restrained warm highlights. Add feathered copper masks only to plausible material zones. Preserve source dimensions and store the transform script plus normalized mask coordinates in provenance.

## Evidence ledger

Record source path/hash, derivative path/hash, transform/prompt, page role, dimensions, colour space, text policy, and unique-use statement for every image. Bind machine checks and the visual inspection note to the exact PDF and render hashes.
